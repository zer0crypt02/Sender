# Frontend Design

Approach this as the design lead at a design studio known for giving every client a distinct visual identity.

## Design principles

- Typography carries the personality of the page
- Choose typefaces deliberately, not default families
- Default to line lengths of less than 80 characters
- Visual structure is information, not decoration
- Use non-user-triggered motion sparingly

## Process

1. Brainstorm a short design plan (color, type, layout, principles)
2. Review that plan against the brief before building
3. Work in two passes: plan, then build

## Restraint

- Spend your boldness in one place
- Let one element be the memorable thing
- Cut any decoration that does not serve the brief
